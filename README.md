# Digital-Twin
A Digital Twin is a digital representation of a real-world object, system, place, or process that is continuously connected to data from the real system.A digital twin is a virtual representation of real-world objects, processes, relationships and behaviors, placed in a geographic context. It can describe the current, past or future state of an asset. The idea began in product manufacturing, where precise models of aircraft or cars were stored in databases for reporting, analysis and later simulation. The paper credits the convergence of GIS, the Internet of Things (IoT) and building information modeling (BIM) with turning it into interactive 3D, and describes GIS plus IoT as a "digital nervous system" for real-time twins.

The stated driver is sustainability. Energy, transportation, water and telecom organizations all need to modernize aging infrastructure while coping with urbanization, decarbonization and digitization
